# Zelp — Logo Kit & Brand Basics

**Zelp** is a place-review and local-discovery app for Zimbabwe: find what's good near you, from the people who actually eat, drink, shop and travel there.

---

## The mark

The mark is a **review pin** — a speech bubble with a dropped point. It carries two ideas at once: a place on a map, and someone saying something about it. Inside sits a knocked-out **Z**, cut on the same 22% stroke weight as the wordmark so the two always look like family.

The geometry is deliberately squared-off rather than a soft teardrop, a nod to the dressed granite of Great Zimbabwe — *dzimba dza mabwe*, houses of stone.

## The wordmark

**ZELP** is drawn, not typed. Geometric caps, flat terminals, a single 22-unit stroke weight, one shared cap height of 100 units. It ships as outlines, so it never depends on a font being installed or licensed.

Do not re-set it in a typeface. Do not re-space it. Use the supplied file.

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Msasa Flame | `#D9502B` | Primary. The logo, primary buttons, active states. |
| Frieze Gold | `#F2B133` | Accent. Star ratings, badges, highlights. Never for body text. |
| Granite Ink | `#15201B` | Text, the one-colour logo, dark surfaces. |
| Soapstone | `#F7F1E7` | Page background, knockouts inside the mark. |
| Msasa Green | `#2E6A52` | Secondary. "Open now", verified, success states. |

Msasa Flame is named for the new leaves that come out red-orange across Zimbabwe's msasa woodland in August — the colour of the country right before the rains. It is deliberately a warm orange-red, not a pure signal red, so the identity doesn't sit in the same lane as other review apps.

App-icon gradient: `#F2B133` → `#E4632C` (42%) → `#C23C1B`, top-left to bottom-right.

**Contrast:** Flame on Soapstone passes AA for large text and UI only. For body copy on Soapstone, use Granite Ink. White on Flame passes AA at 18px+/bold; use Ink on Flame for smaller type.

## Typography

- **Display / headings:** Outfit, SemiBold–Bold, tracking −2%. Geometric enough to sit beside the wordmark without competing.
- **Body / UI:** Inter, Regular and Medium. Reliable at small sizes on cheap Android hardware, which is what most of the audience is holding.
- **Numerals & data:** Inter with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` for ratings, prices and review counts.

Both are open-source. Fallback stack: `Outfit, "Segoe UI", system-ui, sans-serif`.

## The chevron frieze

The pattern is the zigzag frieze that runs along the top of the Great Enclosure at Great Zimbabwe. It is the identity's one piece of ornament, so it earns its keep only when used at scale and with room to breathe: a band across the top of a poster, the empty-state illustration, packaging, the back of a card.

Never run it behind text. Never use it inside the logo's clear space.

## Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space:** the height of the wordmark's cap (the "Z") on all four sides. Nothing enters it — no type, no rules, no photo edges.
- **Minimum size, horizontal lockup:** 120px wide on screen, 30mm in print.
- **Minimum size, icon alone:** 24px. Below that, use the favicon artwork, which is drawn heavier.
- The stacked lockup's tagline is set in live text. If you scale it below 90px wide, delete the tagline rather than let it fill in.

## Don't

- Don't recolour the mark outside the palette, including "just for this one campaign."
- Don't add a drop shadow, bevel, outer glow or outline.
- Don't stretch, condense, rotate or skew any part of it.
- Don't place the full-colour logo on Msasa Flame, on a busy photo, or on any mid-tone — use the white version.
- Don't rebuild the lockup by hand; the icon-to-wordmark spacing is fixed.
- Don't put the mark inside another shape, ring or badge.

## Voice, briefly

Plain, specific, local. Say "Open till 10pm" not "Extended hours available." Say "3 people reviewed this week" not "Trending now." Zimbabwean English and Shona/Ndebele place names are spelled the way people write them, not anglicised.

## One practical note

"Zelp" sits close to an existing registered mark in the same category. This kit deliberately shares nothing with that brand's trade dress — different colour, different mark, different letterforms — but before you spend money on signage or file an application, it's worth a clearance search with a Zimbabwean trade mark attorney (ZIPO registration) and a check of the .co.zw and .com domains.
